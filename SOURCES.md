## Attribution and licensing scope

The project's software license does not relicense the source editions, translations, editorial material, or third-party software. Refer to the linked source pages and the notices accompanying the downloaded files for their applicable terms. Retain those notices when distributing source material.

Rousseau and the credited translators are the authors of the underlying texts and translations, not authors of this project's software. Generated answers, extracted graph relationships, and community reports are project outputs; they should not be presented as verbatim passages from Rousseau unless checked against the source text.

## Corpus provenance

These links identify the source editions. Exact download dates, selected download formats, file checksums, and preprocessing steps have not yet been recorded in this attribution file. Consequently, this file alone does not identify the exact bytes used to build the published indexes.

For a reproducible release, record the downloaded filename and format, retrieval date, checksum, and any removal of headers, footers, notes, or duplicated material for each input file. Preserve the distinction between the original downloads and the prepared indexing inputs.
